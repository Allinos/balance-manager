01 · Short answer

Will DocGen sell in India?

Verdict

Yes, to a clear group of buyers: small businesses that need professional GST documents but not full accounting software.

The market is huge and still half offline, and DocGen costs about one-third of the popular billing apps. The hard part is trust, not the product. Nobody knows DocGen yet, and free tools exist. So your first 50 customers will come from people: CAs, shop visits, WhatsApp and referrals. Ads come later, to multiply what already works.

The plan in one minute

  1. Sell to one group first: traders, distributors, contractors and service firms whose CA keeps the books in Tally.
  2. Spend ₹0 in the first month: visit shops, win CAs and computer shops as partners, build your WhatsApp and Reels.
  3. Make it urgent: a founding price of ₹799 for the first 100 customers, and a Diwali offer that ends on 8 November 2026.
  4. When you have ₹1,000–2,000, run one click-to-WhatsApp video ad for 10 days. Treat it as a test that teaches you your cost per customer.
  5. Reinvest about 30% of each month's sales into the channel that brought the cheapest customers. Move to the next stage only when the numbers say so.

Spending rule

Never spend money you need to live on. From Stage 2, spend about 30% of last month's sales on marketing. Raise the budget only after your cost per customer has stayed under your target for two weeks.

04 · What you are selling

Be clear about what DocGen is, and is not

Selling the right thing to the right person avoids refunds and bad reviews.

What DocGen does

  • 18 document types: GST invoices, quotations, proforma invoices, delivery challans, purchase orders, receipts, credit and debit notes, bill of supply and more.
  • 4 print templates, including Tally-style Professional and Standard formats.
  • CGST, SGST and IGST worked out automatically, HSN/SAC, amount in words, logo, signature, bank details and a UPI QR code on the invoice.
  • Works without internet. Data stays on the customer's computer or phone.
  • One license covers 1 computer and 2 phones (DocGen Mobile installs from the browser).
  • Turn a quotation into an invoice in one click; track status from draft to paid.
  • Monthly sales data as a CSV file with party GSTIN, state and tax split, ready for the CA.
  • 30-day free trial on the computer; backup to a file.

What it does not do (say this honestly)

  • No stock or inventory, no accounting ledgers, no GST return filing.
  • No e-invoice (IRN) or e-way bill generation. E-invoicing is only compulsory above ₹5 crore turnover, so most small businesses don't need it.
  • Not a restaurant or café POS (tables, kitchen orders). Send those buyers to Reynrel's POS and inventory software.
Prices today

1 year ₹1,250 · 2 years ₹2,250 · 5 years ₹4,999. One payment per period, no auto-renewal.

Trends working in your favour

How small Indian businesses decide to buy

  • They trust people more than ads: their CA, a friend, a supplier, the computer shop.
  • They want to see it working on their own bills before paying. A 5-minute demo sells better than any ad.
  • They talk on WhatsApp, not email or forms. Reply fast, in their language.
  • They compare price per month. "₹104 a month" feels smaller than "₹1,250".
  • They prefer one payment to subscriptions. "No auto-debit" builds trust.
  • They fear losing data and complicated software. "Works offline, your data stays with you, ready in 5 minutes" answers both.
  • Many prefer Hindi or their regional language. Hinglish works well in ads.

11.1 CA and tax consultant partners

One CA advises 30–200 small businesses, and a CA's word beats any ad. India has about 4 lakh CAs, plus many GST practitioners and tax consultants.

The partner offer
  • A free DocGen license for the CA's office.
  • ₹250 by UPI for every client who buys (or a free year for one of their clients after 3 sales).
  • A personal tracking link, so they can see their clients' sales.
  • You install and train their clients. The CA only recommends.
  1. List 50 CAs and tax consultants near you (Google Maps, JustDial, LinkedIn, ICAI branch directory).
  2. Pick your timing. CAs are busiest until the end of October (audits and income-tax returns) and around the 11th and 20th of each month (GST return dates). Contact them from 1 November, ideally in the last or first week of a month.
  3. Send a short WhatsApp or LinkedIn message (section 14), then visit with a one-page sheet and a sample invoice.
  4. Give a 5-minute demo on your phone. Show the sales CSV export; that is what CAs care about.
  5. Install DocGen on their office computer for free. Ask: "Which 3 clients would benefit most?" Offer to call them.
  6. Pay commissions on time, every month. Send a short monthly update: "4 of your clients now use DocGen."
  7. Later, offer a free 30-minute talk at an ICAI branch or CA study circle: "GST invoice mistakes clients make".

Target: 20 CAs contacted a week → 5 meetings → 2 active partners. Ten active partners with 3 clients each gives you 30 customers.

What this means

The first ₹1,500 will roughly pay for itself, not make a profit. Its real value is learning: which hook gets cheap chats, which city replies, what people ask. Don't judge ads by the first test alone.

12.4 While the ad runs

  • Change nothing for the first 3 days; Meta is learning who to show it to.
  • Check daily: money spent, cost per conversation, chats, trials, sales.
  • After day 4, pause the video with the higher cost per conversation.
  • Stop rule: if a conversation costs more than ₹120 after ₹600 spent, stop and change the hook of the video, not the targeting.
  • Grow rule: if a conversation costs under ₹60 and at least 1 in 15 chats buys, raise the budget by 20% every 3 days.
  • Reply within 5 minutes. A slow reply throws away the chat you paid for.

12.5 Next steps at later stages

  • Stage 2: retarget people who watched half of a video (Custom audience → Video viewers) with a testimonial or offer. Add a second city. Run Diwali and new-year offer ads in season.
  • Stage 3: add the Meta Pixel to the website and retarget visitors; use lookalike audiences once you have 100+ customers. Start Google Search ads on exact keywords such as "gst billing software", "quotation software" and "invoice software for small business". Software clicks cost about ₹50–300 each in India, so use exact match and a tight daily cap.
  • Stage 4: separate campaigns per state and language; testimonial ads always running.

12.6 Mistakes that burn small budgets

  • Pressing "Boost post" with the "profile visits" goal.
  • Choosing the "Sales" goal with a tiny budget. Meta can't learn without many purchases a week.
  • Targeting all of India, in English.
  • Changing ads every day.
  • Long, slow videos without captions.
  • Sending clicks to a website when WhatsApp is where you close sales.
  • Not tracking where each sale came from.
  • Forgetting the season: October to December ad prices are 30–60% higher. Lean on free channels and offers then, and advertise only with a ready Diwali video.

RiskWhat to do
Free tools from big brandsTrial + demo + offline and privacy + personal service. Stay focused on your group of buyers.
People don't trust a new brandReviews, video testimonials, your face and phone number, the clear refund policy on the website, "Made in India by Reynrel".
Ad account blockedVerify your business with Meta, follow ad rules, avoid big claims, use two-factor login, don't launch many new ads at once.
WhatsApp number bannedNo bulk messages to strangers. Broadcasts only to saved contacts. Be polite in groups.
